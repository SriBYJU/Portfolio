06 / About

I don’t really have one lane. I tend to find a problem, open way too many tabs, and keep going until there’s something real enough for another person to use.

That’s how a college-planning frustration became Scholark, finance curiosity became Capital Mastery, smart-glasses research became OpenLens, and questions about data ownership became DataShadow.

I’m still in high school, which mostly means I’m learning while building in public—and getting comfortable with the fact that those two things happen at the same time.
